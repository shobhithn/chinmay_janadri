About the Firm

A single point of accountability for complex businesses.

We are Chartered Accountants with a team of qualified and motivated professionals, offering integrated, one-stop services across audit, management advisory, taxation consultation and transaction advisory work.

Our founding partner recognised a huge vacuum in the Indian professional services sector — firms with deep expertise in independent domains, but without a holistic approach to a client’s business. Chinmay Janadri & Company was built to fill that vacuum: to provide an integrated solution to a client’s business rather than advice given in isolation.

Every engagement is guided by that same purpose — enhancing our clients’ organisational and economic well-being through timely, value-adding, and technically sound services in direct taxation, GST, corporate law, statutory audit and regulatory compliance.
