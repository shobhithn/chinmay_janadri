About the Firm

Two partners. One holistic approach.

We opened our doors on 13 January 2015 with a simple premise: a client shouldn’t have to juggle three different consultants for one business problem. CA Manik Mandaknalli runs the Kalaburagi office; CA Chinmay R Janadri runs Sedam. Between them, every engagement gets a partner’s direct attention, not a hand-off to whoever’s free.

That grew out of hands-on work across banking, manufacturing, retail, aviation and leasing — sectors where the paperwork looks similar on the surface but the risks rarely are.
